How to Use ContrastPlay

Gentle visual play for 0–6+ months

Getting Started

Hold the screen or printed card about 8–12 inches (20–30 cm) from your baby's eyes—right in a newborn's sharpest focus range. Pause on one pattern for 10–20 seconds so they can lock on, then double-tap for a new card or tap Play to cycle gently on a timer.

Eye Tracking with Motion

Tap Motion to add calm, predictable movement for visual pursuit practice:

  • Gentle Drift — slow side-to-side glide that encourages smooth horizontal eye tracking across the midline.
  • Breathe — soft rhythmic zoom that helps babies hold focus on bold edges.
  • Slow Spin — unhurried rotation that turns mandalas and optical patterns into soothing circular tracking.

Why High Contrast Helps

In the first months, a baby's retina and visual pathways are developing rapidly. Stark black and white edges send the clearest signal to the brain, helping build visual focus, attention span, and optic nerve strength. Around 2–4 months, babies begin distinguishing bold red clearly—making black, white, and red patterns great for tummy time and alert awake windows.

Keep sessions short and calm (a few minutes at a time), and follow your baby's cues—wide eyes and stillness mean engaged, while turning away or yawning means it's time for a break.
